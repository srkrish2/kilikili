  • Say the word once or twice, the way you'd say it at home, then tap “I said it”.
  • Don't look at or point to the right picture. Kids read your eyes.
  • Stop while he still wants more. One 5–10 minute trip a day is plenty.

What he understands

Each first tap updates an estimate of whether he knows the word, corrected for lucky guesses (1 in 3 with three pictures). A word counts as known once that estimate passes 90% and he has got it right on two different days. Retries after a miss don't count.
